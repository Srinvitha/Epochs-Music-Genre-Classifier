# Music-Generation Prompt Design

## Purpose
This document helps Epochs convert a creative brief into a prompt for a separate music-generation system. It does not mean Epochs itself generates audio.

## Prompt recipe
Use the following fields where relevant:
- **Goal:** background loop, intro, transition, short soundtrack, or full track.
- **Duration:** requested length; state that exact duration may depend on the tool.
- **Style:** one primary genre or sound world, with at most a small number of supporting influences.
- **Mood:** emotional adjectives plus the intended scene.
- **Sound palette:** instruments or sound roles, not just a list of genre names.
- **Rhythm/tempo feel:** pulse, groove, or approximate BPM if helpful.
- **Arrangement:** opening, development, peak, ending/loop.
- **Vocals:** instrumental, vocal texture, or lyrics only if desired and supported.
- **Mix character:** dry/intimate, spacious, distorted, clean, etc.
- **Constraints:** no vocals, no abrupt cutoff, loopable ending, avoid overcrowding.

## Example: 15-second dark cinematic story
“Create a short instrumental cue for a mysterious night-time character reveal. Dark, spacious, and tense, with a low sustained drone, a restrained pulsing bass, a sparse two-note synth motif, and a gradual filter build. Around the final third, introduce one impactful hit and let a controlled reverb tail decay. Keep the arrangement uncluttered and leave room for dialogue or visual effects. No intelligible vocals. Aim for a short, coherent clip with a deliberate ending.”

## Example: nostalgic photo montage
“Create a short instrumental background cue for a nostalgic photo montage. Use mellow keys, a soft plucked motif, gentle bass, subtle texture, and a relaxed pulse. Let the motif evolve slightly rather than adding many layers. Keep the mix warm but clear, with a gentle resolving ending or a loop-friendly boundary. No lead vocals.”

## Example: dark synthwave reinterpretation
“Create a dark, retro-futuristic instrumental with a steady electronic pulse, sustained analog-style pads, a memorable synth lead, and a deep but controlled bass. Begin spaciously, introduce the groove, then reveal the lead motif and end with a cinematic tail. Keep the layers distinct and avoid excessive distortion.”

## Prompt quality checks
- Is there one clear primary style?
- Are mood and intended use described?
- Is the sound palette coherent?
- Does the arrangement fit the duration?
- Is the ending specified?
- Are constraints compatible?
- Are exact technical requirements stated as preferences rather than guaranteed outcomes?

## Boundaries
Do not imitate a living artist's exact style. Instead, translate the request into general musical attributes such as instrumentation, mood, rhythmic feel, production era, and arrangement. Do not claim a prompt guarantees a particular output.
