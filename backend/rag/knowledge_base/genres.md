# Epochs Genre Knowledge

## Hip-Hop
A genre commonly associated with rhythmic emphasis, beat-driven structure, rap/vocal delivery, and characteristic timbral patterns. Audio-only genre classification may confuse Hip-Hop with other rhythmically prominent genres.

## Pop
A broad commercial genre with highly varied instrumentation, production styles, vocal arrangements, and rhythmic structures. Its breadth makes it difficult to separate reliably using a compact handcrafted feature representation.

## Folk
A broad family of music traditions often associated with acoustic instrumentation, distinctive melodic/harmonic patterns, and culturally specific performance styles. The FMA label is broader than any single folk tradition.

## Experimental
A broad category for music that may depart from conventional genre structures, instrumentation, or production techniques. Its diversity can make it difficult for a classifier to learn one compact acoustic signature.

## Rock
A broad family of music commonly characterized by prominent rhythm-section activity and guitar-oriented or similarly energetic instrumentation, but with substantial variation across substyles.

## International
A broad FMA category containing music associated with traditions outside the other labels. It is especially heterogeneous, so acoustic overlap with other genres is expected.

## Electronic
Music where electronically produced or processed sound is central to the production. Tempo, spectral characteristics, timbral texture, and rhythmic regularity may contribute to classification, but none alone determines the genre.

## Instrumental
A label emphasizing the absence or reduced prominence of vocals rather than one specific musical style. It can therefore overlap acoustically with many other genres.
